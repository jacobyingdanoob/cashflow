# Cashflow 1.1.0

The browser build and portable Windows x86 app contain the same updated game. Download either package from the [v1.1.0 release](https://github.com/jacobyingdanoob/cashflow/releases/tag/v1.1.0).

## Markets and trading

- Stocks, property and businesses now have persistent prices, interactive market charts and a round-change ticker. Weighted market shocks, three-round economic trends and ordinary round movements affect all players equally.
- Deep declines can trigger shared discount opportunities and temporary market depressions. Saved queues and market timers resume without rerolling or applying movements twice.
- Sell stocks anytime. List property and businesses at locked quick-sale prices, with closings after one to three rounds and free cancellation. Fees, profit tax, early mortgage payoff penalties and closing shortfalls appear in the transaction previews.

## Borrowing and opponents

- New-game bank loans charge 2% monthly. Type 1 purchase loans charge 1% monthly and complete together with the acquisition. Financed acquisitions use rising down-payment tiers, and new debt must meet a 55% debt-to-income limit.
- Choose one to eight NPCs, with four by default. Everyone begins with the same role and finances; conservative, aggressive, speculative and balanced personalities make different choices under the same rules. Players receive distinct dreams.

## Guidance, history and reviews

- First-encounter tutorials explain board spaces and the three asset types. Lessons queue, pause play, survive refresh and stay dismissed once acknowledged.
- The rulebook, market references and clearer expandable cost breakdowns explain decisions. Inspect other players' finances without changing them.
- Transaction pages draw from the full financial timeline, with round labels, ordering and ten entries per page.
- Risk-focused coaching considers debt reserves and emergency buffers. Personal elimination reviews preserve exit finances while remaining NPCs can finish the match. Compact notifications retain filtering, clearing and detail views.

## Downloads and upgrades

- **Browser:** [Cashflow-1.1.0-web.zip](https://github.com/jacobyingdanoob/cashflow/releases/download/v1.1.0/Cashflow-1.1.0-web.zip). Extract and run the included `node serve.cjs`, or use a static HTTP host. Keep the same site address to access existing browser saves. See the [browser guide](../web.md).
- **Windows:** [Cashflow-1.1.0-windows-x86.exe](https://github.com/jacobyingdanoob/cashflow/releases/download/v1.1.0/Cashflow-1.1.0-windows-x86.exe). Portable, offline, 32-bit x86 for Windows 10+; also runs on 64-bit Windows. The app remains unsigned. Close v1.0.0 before launching the new executable; saves remain in `%APPDATA%\Cashflow`. See the [Windows guide](../windows-x86.md).
- **Verification:** `SHA256SUMS.txt` on the release lists hashes for both downloads.

Older saves remain supported. Matches created before persistent markets keep their earlier market and cycle rules, rates and pending transactions; start a new match for all new rules. Browser and desktop saves stay separate. Newer saves should not be opened in the old executable.

## Validation

All 221 game tests and 72 browser tests passed, together with the production build. The actual x86 portable executable passed offline launch, gameplay, save restoration and reference-page loading. A save created by the original v1.0.0 executable was reopened successfully in v1.1.0 with its player data and legacy market rules preserved. Both Windows launcher and runtime have verified 32-bit x86 PE headers; runtime checks used 64-bit Windows 11. The extracted web archive also passed asset-integrity, local-server, gameplay, reference-page and save-reload checks with external requests blocked.
